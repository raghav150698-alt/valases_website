01 / ARCHITECTURE

Controls built into the operating model.

Security and privacy are treated as product behavior: who can see evidence, where it is assigned, what is recorded, and how decisions are reviewed.

DATA

Organization isolation

Hiring records are associated with an organization and API access resolves the authenticated organization context server-side.

REGION

Regional assignment

Customer organizations can be provisioned against approved Mumbai or Tokyo deployment foundations and the assignment source is recorded.

ACCESS

Role-based permissions

Administrative, recruiting, interviewing, payroll, viewing, and release responsibilities are separated by workflow permissions.

SECRETS

Server-side credentials

Database, storage, email, and payment secrets remain server-side and are never exposed through public browser variables.

STORAGE

Private document architecture

Candidate documents are designed for private object storage, organization-prefixed keys, short-lived access, scanning, and retention controls.

AUDIT

Actor-linked history

Workflow changes, evidence review, compliance checks, score adjustments, and offer actions retain actor and event context.

PROCTORING

Advisory integrity signals

AI-assisted proctoring signals support human review and are not treated as an automatic rejection, hiring decision, or final finding.

AI GOVERNANCE

Assistance with accountable review

AI can draft first-pass feedback, organize screening evidence, assist integrity review, and prepare offer language. Authorized people review outputs and remain responsible for decisions and release.

RETENTION

Customer-defined requirements

Candidate, attempt, proctoring, document, export, and audit retention requirements are agreed during implementation.

02 / CUSTOMER REVIEW

What we provide during due diligence.

Enterprise customers can request architecture details, data-flow review, subprocessor information, retention planning, access-role mapping, incident contacts, and deployment acceptance evidence.

Control availability can vary by deployment and implementation stage. The customer order form and implementation plan identify the exact region, retention, integrations, support, and security responsibilities for the rollout.
