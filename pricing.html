QUESTIONS

Commercial clarity.

Is pricing charged in any currency other than USD?

The public commercial model is denominated in United States dollars. Applicable taxes and payment terms appear in the customer order form.

Are assessment invitations included?

Each plan includes an agreed usage allowance. Higher volumes and specialized work environments are scoped transparently before activation.

Can we choose our deployment region?

Regional requirements are reviewed during onboarding. Available regional foundations and the approved assignment are recorded in the customer implementation plan.

Can we start with a controlled pilot?

Yes. A pilot can focus on selected roles, assessors, candidate volume, and acceptance criteria before a broader rollout.
