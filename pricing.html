QUESTIONS

Commercial clarity.

How does the three-month introductory price work?

Hire Core is $99 per month for months one through three and $119 per month for the remaining nine months. Hire Growth is $249 per month for months one through three and $299 per month for the remaining nine months. Both require a 12-month commitment.

Are assessments included in Hire Core or Hire Growth?

Every new Hire subscription includes 30 complimentary assessment attempts available during the first month. The allocation across assessment formats is agreed before launch. Continued usage is charged according to the selected format and expected volume.

How is additional assessment usage priced?

Different assessment formats require different delivery environments and processing. The applicable usage schedule is shared during the commercial discussion and recorded in the order form before assessment activation.

Can Indian businesses purchase these plans?

Yes. The same introductory annual offer is available to eligible new customers in India. Public pricing remains in USD, while applicable taxes, invoicing, payment mechanics, and implementation scope are confirmed in the order form.

Is a month-to-month option available?

Yes. Hire Core is $149 per month and Hire Growth is $349 per month without an annual commitment. The three-month introductory offer applies only to annual plans.

Can we choose our deployment region?

Regional requirements are reviewed during onboarding. Available regional foundations and the approved assignment are recorded in the customer implementation plan.

Can we start with a controlled pilot?

Yes. A pilot can focus on selected roles, assessors, candidate volume, and acceptance criteria before a broader rollout.
