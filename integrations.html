CONNECTION STANDARD

Every live connection requires evidence.

01

Approved authorization

Provider-specific application registration, redirect URIs, minimum scopes, encrypted credentials, and accountable administrators.

02

Reliable event handling

Webhook signature checks, idempotency, retries, reconciliation, and an operational path for failed synchronization.

03

Visible data scope

The organization records the approved external account and sync scope without asking users to paste passwords or tokens into the workspace.
