02 / CONTROLS

Designed around time, integrity, and review.

Every assessment can communicate expectations clearly while protecting the evidence needed by the hiring team.

T

Time-centric delivery

Use whole-assessment or per-question timing, configured duration, availability windows, visible countdowns, and secure attempt state.

P

Consent-led proctoring

Run camera and environment preflight, then collect fullscreen, visibility, focus, phone, gaze, and multiple-person signals for human review.

S

Explainable scoring

Score MCQs with clear keys and task environments with weighted checkpoints, tolerances, expected values, and recorded reviewer changes.

B

Assessment builder

Create, preview, validate, publish, version, duplicate, archive, and reuse role-relevant assessment definitions.

D

Secure delivery

Issue candidate-specific links with expiration, resend and revoke controls, autosave, recovery, and duplicate-safe submission.

R

Reviewer workspace

Inspect answers, task evidence, checkpoint matches, activity events, advisory integrity signals, and override reasons in context.

03 / HUMAN CONTROL

Integrity signals are evidence.
Not a verdict.

Valases does not treat an advisory proctoring event as an automatic employment decision. Reviewers remain responsible for interpreting context and reaching the outcome.
