Our position

Valases is the enterprise hiring operating system built for real-work signal, explainable evidence, and accountable human selection.

01 / THE GAP

Most hiring stacks move data.
They lose reasoning.

An ATS tracks stages. A testing tool produces a score. Calendars schedule meetings. Documents carry offers. The hiring team is left to reconstruct the actual decision across tabs, messages, and memory.

01

Context survives every handoff

The role brief, required skills, candidate evidence, assessment results, interview scorecards, and decision checks remain connected.

02

Work replaces proxies

Candidates can demonstrate spreadsheet, coding, accounting, tax, analytical, and case-based capability inside relevant work environments.

03

AI supports, people decide

AI creates first-pass feedback, organizes evidence, assists integrity review, and drafts offer language. People validate outputs and own every hiring decision.

04

Scores remain explainable

Question keys and weighted checkpoints show what was evaluated, what matched, and where reviewer judgment changed an outcome.

05

Governance lives in the workflow

Role permissions, compliance checks, actor-linked events, controlled offer release, and regional assignment are part of daily work.

06

Candidates see a coherent process

Clear instructions, consent before capture, autosave, secure sessions, and human-reviewed outcomes create a more respectful experience.
