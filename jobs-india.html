CANDIDATE GUIDE / INDIA

Applying for a job
through Valases?

Employers use Valases to receive applications, deliver role-relevant assessments, and coordinate hiring evidence. This guide explains what candidates applying for jobs in India can expect.

Valases is a hiring technology platform, not a public job board. Open roles and application links are published or sent by the hiring employer.

APPLICATIONS IN INDIA

A clear path from application to assessment.

Your experience depends on the employer and role, but a Valases-powered process is designed to show the purpose, timing, required information, save state, and next action.

01

Employer-issued access

Use the career-page or invitation link supplied by the employer. Candidate links may be role-specific, time-limited, and personal to you.

02

Job information

Read the employer’s role description, location, working arrangement, requirements, and application instructions before submitting.

03

Candidate privacy

Review what information is requested, why it is needed, and any assessment or proctoring notice before you actively consent.

04

Protected progress

Assessment answers and task state can be autosaved, with visible confirmation and recovery support where configured.

05

Role-relevant work

You may receive coding, spreadsheet, accounting, tax, MCQ, or case-study work based on the requirements of the vacancy.

06

Human-reviewed outcomes

Automated scoring, AI-assisted feedback, and advisory integrity events remain inputs for the employer’s authorized reviewers.

BEFORE AN ONLINE ASSESSMENT

Prepare the environment, then focus on the work.

Use the preflight information in your invitation and allow enough uninterrupted time for the assigned task.

  • Confirm the deadline, duration, browser, and tools
  • Use a stable internet connection and supported device
  • Read camera or screen-capture notices before consenting
  • Keep the employer’s support contact available
  • Submit only through the employer-issued Valases link
CANDIDATE RESOURCES

Know how your assessment is delivered.

Read the candidate privacy notice, proctoring notice, and accessibility commitment. For a role-specific question, contact the employer named in your invitation.
