THE JOURNEY

Know what will happen before the clock starts.

The candidate receives role-linked access and sees the assessment purpose, duration, tools, privacy notice, and any proctoring expectations before beginning.

01

Candidate-specific access

Issued links can expire, be resent, or be revoked without providing access to internal hiring information.

02

Active consent

Required notices appear before a proctored session and consent must be actively provided before capture begins.

03

Clear preflight

Tool, camera, browser, and environment checks identify issues before they interrupt the assessment.

04

Protected progress

Answers and task state are autosaved, recoverable, and visibly confirmed throughout the session.

05

Focused workspace

The task, source material, timer, navigation, save state, and final action remain legible and predictable.

06

Human-reviewed outcome

Candidates are not shown an immediate employment result and advisory integrity events are reviewed in context.
