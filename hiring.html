HIRING PLATFORM QUESTIONS

More than applicant tracking.

A hiring platform should help a team define the role, manage applicants, evaluate job-relevant evidence, coordinate interviews, approve offers, and preserve the handoff into onboarding.

01

What is a hiring platform?

It is a shared system for recruiters, hiring managers, interviewers, and operations teams to manage the complete hiring process with consistent records, ownership, and permissions.

02

How is it different from an ATS?

An applicant tracking system primarily organizes applications and pipeline stages. Valases connects that tracking layer to practical assessments, structured interviews, decisions, offers, and onboarding readiness.

03

Can it support global teams?

Yes. Teams can configure workflows, access, assessment delivery, and implementation boundaries for distributed hiring. Regional requirements are reviewed during implementation rather than assumed.
